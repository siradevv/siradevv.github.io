TGCal Support

Help with importing rosters, the calendar, Partner, Friends and live flight info.

Importing a roster

  • Open the roster PDF in Mail, LINE or Files, tap Share and choose TGCal. Or tap + on the Roster tab and pick the PDF.
  • Check the flights on the review screen, then tap Add to Calendar (or Save Roster if you keep your roster in TGCal only).
  • Got an updated roster? Import it again. TGCal replaces that month and shows what changed. You won't get duplicate events.
  • Use the original roster PDF. Scans and photos can't be read.
  • If the review screen shows warnings, please email us the roster so we can fix it.

Calendar

  • Flights go to TGCal's own TGCal calendar on your iPhone. Your own events are never touched.
  • To keep your roster in TGCal only, turn off TGCal → Settings → Add to Calendar. You can remove the flights already added or keep them.
  • TGCal needs Full Access to calendars: Settings → Privacy & Security → Calendars → TGCal.
  • Flight times are shown in each airport's local time, just like on the roster.

Partner and Friends

  • Sign in with Apple on the People tab. Importing and the calendar work without an account.
  • Partner: the person who flies invites from People → Partner; the other opens the link or enters the code. You both see the same roster and your days off together.
  • Friends and groups: tap + on the People tab to invite friends, create a group or join one with its code, then see who's free each day.
  • To delete your account and everything on the TGCal server: People → your card → Delete Account.

Widgets, Apple Watch and live flight info

  • To add the widget, touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget and choose TGCal. On the Lock Screen: touch and hold it, tap Customise, then the box under the clock.
  • The Apple Watch app shows the same as your iPhone and updates from it.
  • Live flight status, alerts and Live Activities need you to sign in (People tab). They're on by default and can be turned off in Settings → Live Flight Info.
