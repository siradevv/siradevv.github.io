TGCal Support

Help with importing rosters, calendars and live flight info.

Importing a roster

  • Open the roster PDF in Mail, LINE or Files, tap Share and choose TGCal. Or tap + on the Roster tab and pick the PDF.
  • Check the flights on the review screen, then tap Add to Calendar.
  • Got an updated roster? Import it again. TGCal replaces that month and shows what changed. You won't get duplicate events.
  • If the review screen shows warnings, please email us the roster so we can fix it.

Calendar

  • TGCal needs Full Access to calendars: Settings → Privacy & Security → Calendars → TGCal.
  • Flights go to the "TG Roster" calendar by default. You can change it in TGCal → Settings → Calendar.
  • Flight times are shown in each airport's local time, just like on the roster.

Widget and live flight info

  • To add the widget, touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget and choose TGCal.
  • Live flight status, alerts and Live Activities need you to sign in (People tab). They're on by default and can be turned off in Settings → Live Flight Info. Everything else works without an account.
