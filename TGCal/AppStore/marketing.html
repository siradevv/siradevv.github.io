TGCal

Thai Airways roster PDF to iPhone calendar, exactly.

What TGCal does

  • Reads your Crew Schedule Slip PDF directly and checks itself against the roster's own block-time total.
  • Adds every flight to your calendar in the airport's local time. Re-importing replaces the month, so there are no duplicates.
  • Shows what changed when an updated roster arrives.
  • Shows your next flight with destination weather, local time and currency.
  • Live flight status, delay alerts and Live Activities on the Lock Screen.
  • "Where Now" widget: flying, on layover or home, and what's next.
  • Logbook: monthly pay estimate, flying hours and top destinations.
  • Reminders 12 and 3 hours before each departure.

Privacy

No account, no ads and no tracking. Your roster is read on your iPhone. If you turn on live flight info, your flight numbers, dates and times, plus a notification address, are sent to the TGCal server so it can check flight status and send alerts. See the Privacy Policy.
