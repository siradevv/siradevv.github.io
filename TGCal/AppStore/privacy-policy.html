Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Overview

TGCal turns your roster PDF into calendar events, on iPhone and on Android. The PDF is read on your device and is never uploaded.

An account is optional: Sign in with Apple (Sign in with Google on Android) is only needed to share your roster with a partner, to see when friends are free, or for live flight info. There is no advertising and no tracking; the iPhone app sends only anonymous usage statistics (see Usage Statistics). TGCal doesn't use your location, and your data is never sold or shared for marketing.

Data Stored on Your Device

  • Your imported rosters and settings.
  • Briefing notes you add.
  • Your sign-in (in the iPhone Keychain, or the app's private storage on Android) and a random ID for this install.
  • Deleting the app removes your rosters, notes and settings.

Your Account

Only if you sign in, the TGCal server keeps:

  • Your Apple user ID, or on Android your Google user ID (a code from Apple or Google, not your email address), and the name you choose.
  • Your profile, if you fill it in: photo, nickname, what you work as, position, training class and birthday (day and month only).
  • The roster you share: each month's flights and duties, as read from your PDF.
  • For friends and groups: who your friends are, and which days you're busy and, unless you turn off Show Where I Fly, the city, flight number and times.
  • Your phones' notification addresses, so your partner's changes and flight alerts reach you.
  • With live flight info on (Settings → Live Flight Info, on unless you turn it off): the flights on your roster, so the server can check them on FlightAware. They're deleted about 3 days after departure, and only you and your partner see their live status.
  • If you see your partner's roster and choose calendars for Free Together: those calendars' events (title, calendar name and times) for the roster's months.
  • Your partner sees your roster (or you see theirs) and your profile. Your friends and members of your groups see your profile and only which days you're busy, and the city, flight number and times if shown. Nobody else sees any of it.
  • Delete Account (People tab → your profile) removes everything the server keeps about you. Signing out removes this phone and its flights.

Calendar Access

TGCal asks for Calendar access when you first add a roster to your calendar or choose calendars for Free Together.

  • TGCal adds events only when you choose to (or when your partner's roster arrives), and only updates or removes events it created itself.
  • If you choose calendars for Free Together, it reads their events to find days you're both free. Those events leave your phone only as described under Your Account.

Notifications

Duty reminders are scheduled on your device. When your partner changes the roster, the TGCal server sends your phone a silent notification so it updates. With live flight info on, the server also sends flight alerts and Live Activity updates. Android phones get no silent notifications: TGCal on Android checks the server when you open it, and every few minutes while one of your flights is near; its flight alerts and live flight notification are made on the phone.

Usage Statistics

The iPhone app sends anonymous usage statistics to TelemetryDeck, so the developer can see whether rosters import correctly and which features are used: for example that a roster was read with no problems, that an airport's time zone is missing (its three-letter code), that the calendar couldn't be updated, or that a screen was opened. Each comes with the app version, iPhone model, iOS version and language, and a random ID made for this install that isn't connected to you or your account.

No names, flight numbers, dates or roster contents are sent. Debug and test copies of the app are marked so they don't count.

Online Services

Some features use online services:

  • Open-Meteo, for weather: receives the coordinates of airports and of points along a flight's route.
  • open.er-api.com, for currency exchange rates: receives no personal data.
  • Apple (iPhone) or Google (Android), for signing in, and for place names such as “Over Taiwan”: receives the aircraft's position during a flight and points along a flight's route.
  • TGCal server: your account and live flight info, as above.
  • FlightAware, used by the TGCal server for live flight status: receives only flight numbers and dates.
  • TelemetryDeck (iPhone), for anonymous usage statistics: as described under Usage Statistics.
