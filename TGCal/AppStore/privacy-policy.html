Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Overview

TGCal turns your roster PDF into iPhone calendar events. The PDF is read on your device and is never uploaded.

An account is optional: Sign in with Apple is only needed to share your roster with a partner or to see when friends are free. There is no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. TGCal doesn't use your location, and your data is never sold or shared for marketing.

Data Stored on Your Device

  • Your imported rosters and settings.
  • Briefing notes you add.
  • Your sign-in (in the iPhone Keychain) and a random ID for this install.
  • Deleting the app removes your rosters, notes and settings.

Your Account

Only if you sign in, the TGCal server keeps:

  • Your Apple user ID (a code from Apple, not your email address) and the name you choose.
  • Your profile, if you fill it in: photo, nickname, what you work as, position, training class and birthday (day and month only).
  • The roster you share: each month's flights and duties, as read from your PDF.
  • For Friends groups: which days you're busy and, unless you turn off Show Where I Fly, the city.
  • Your phones' notification addresses, so your partner's changes reach you.
  • If you see your partner's roster and choose calendars for Free Together: those calendars' events (title, calendar name and times) for the roster's months.
  • Your partner sees your roster (or you see theirs) and your profile. Members of your Friends groups see your profile and only which days you're busy, and the city if shown. Nobody else sees any of it.
  • Delete Account (Settings → your profile) removes everything the server keeps about you.

Calendar Access

TGCal asks for Calendar access when you first add a roster to your calendar or choose calendars for Free Together.

  • TGCal adds events only when you choose to (or when your partner's roster arrives), and only updates or removes events it created itself.
  • If you choose calendars for Free Together, it reads their events to find days you're both free. Those events leave your phone only as described under Your Account.

Notifications

Duty reminders are scheduled on your device. When your partner changes the roster, the TGCal server sends your phone a silent notification so it updates. With live flight info on, the server also sends flight alerts and Live Activity updates.

Online Services

Some features use online services:

  • Open-Meteo, for weather: receives the coordinates of airports and of points along a flight's route.
  • open.er-api.com, for currency exchange rates: receives no personal data.
  • Apple, for Sign in with Apple, and for place names such as “Over Taiwan” (live flight info): receives the aircraft's position during a flight.
  • TGCal server: your account, as above. With live flight info on, it also receives the flights from your roster and this install's notification addresses to check FlightAware; those flights are deleted about 3 days after departure.
  • FlightAware, used by the TGCal server for live flight status: receives only flight numbers and dates.
