Privacy Policy

Overview

TGCal helps you import roster PDFs, manage flight details, and add flights to Apple Calendar. Your roster PDF is read on your device and is never uploaded.

Data Stored on Your Device

  • Imported roster month data.
  • Briefing notes you add.
  • No account is required. Your roster stays on your device, except for the live flight info described below.

Calendar Access

TGCal requests Calendar permission only when you use calendar features.

  • TGCal adds events only after you explicitly choose to add them.
  • When updating an existing calendar, TGCal may remove and re-add TGCal-created events for the selected month.

Notifications

Duty reminders are scheduled on your device. With live flight info on, the TGCal server also sends flight alerts (delays, takeoff, landing) and Live Activity updates. You can turn either off in Settings at any time.

Online Services

Some features call third-party services:

  • Open-Meteo for weather.
  • Exchange-rate API for THB conversion.
  • TGCal server (only if you enter a server code for live flight info): receives the flights from your roster (flight numbers, airports, dates and times) and this device's notification addresses, to check FlightAware and send flight alerts and Live Activity updates. Past flights are deleted after about 3 days. Nothing else is sent.
  • FlightAware, used by the TGCal server for live flight status such as takeoff and landing times, gate and aircraft (only the flight number and date are sent).
