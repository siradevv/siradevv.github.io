Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Overview

TGCal turns your roster PDF into iPhone calendar events. The PDF is read on your device and is never uploaded.

There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. TGCal doesn't use your location, and your data is never sold or shared for marketing.

Data Stored on Your Device

  • Your imported rosters and settings.
  • Briefing notes you add.
  • The server code for live flight info (in the iPhone Keychain) and a random ID for this install.
  • Deleting the app removes your rosters, notes and settings.

Calendar Access

TGCal asks for Calendar access when you first add a roster to your calendar or choose calendars for Free Together. Everything below happens on your device; calendar events are never sent to the TGCal server or anyone else.

  • TGCal adds events only when you choose to, and only updates or removes events it created itself (when you update or delete a month).
  • It reads TGCal's events in your roster calendar to keep two phones in step through a shared iCloud calendar. Shared calendars sync through Apple's iCloud under your Apple account.
  • If you choose calendars for Free Together, it reads their events to find days you're both free.

Notifications

Duty reminders are scheduled on your device. With live flight info on, the TGCal server also sends flight alerts (delays, takeoff, landing) and Live Activity updates. You can turn either off in Settings at any time.

Online Services

Some features use online services:

  • Open-Meteo, for weather: receives the coordinates of airports and of points along a flight's route.
  • open.er-api.com, for currency exchange rates: receives no personal data.
  • Apple, for place names such as “Over Taiwan”: receives the aircraft's position during a flight.
  • TGCal server (only if you enter a server code for live flight info): receives the flights from your roster (flight numbers, airports, dates and times), this install's random ID, its notification addresses and whether alerts are on, to check FlightAware and send flight alerts and Live Activity updates. Flights are deleted about 3 days after departure. Nothing else is sent. To have everything deleted, email us.
  • FlightAware, used by the TGCal server for live flight status such as takeoff and landing times, gate and aircraft: receives only flight numbers and dates.
